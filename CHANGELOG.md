# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Reads bill SMS from bank and biller sender IDs on the phone: credit cards,
  loan EMIs, electricity, water, gas, postpaid mobile, broadband, prepaid
  expiry and insurance premiums.
- Merges a statement and its reminders into one bill.
- Reminders 3 days before, 1 day before and on the due day (adjustable).
- Upcoming list with this month's total; mark as paid; add bills by hand,
  with monthly repeat.
- Works without SMS access as a manual bill list.
- No internet permission. Delete all data from Settings.
